# DAD DAD Workbench — Setting It Up

A design program for the workshop. This page gets it onto your Mac.
You only have to do this once.

---

## Putting Workbench on your Mac

**1.** Open the folder you were given. Inside it is an icon called **Workbench**.

**2.** Drag the Workbench icon onto the **Applications** folder shown beside it.
The Mac copies it across. That is the whole installation.

**3.** Open **Launchpad** (the rocket in the Dock) and find Workbench there.

---

## Opening it the first time

The very first time, do it this way. Only the first time.

**4.** In Launchpad, hold the **Control** key and click the Workbench icon.
(A right-click does the same thing, if you use a mouse with two buttons.)

**5.** A short list appears. Choose **Open**.

**6.** A box appears saying Workbench is from an **unidentified developer**.
That sentence only means the program was made by your son rather than bought
from Apple's store. It is safe.

**7.** In that box, click **Open**.

Workbench starts. The Mac now trusts it, and will not ask again.

**8.** From now on, open Workbench like anything else — from Launchpad, or
click and hold its icon in the Dock and choose **Keep in Dock** so it is
always one click away.

---

## How your projects are saved

There is no Save button, and nothing to remember.

- Your work is kept as you go. Every change is written down straight away.
- Each project is a file ending in **.workbench**. Keep it wherever you like —
  the Desktop, a folder, a memory stick.
- You can email a .workbench file to someone.
- Double-click a .workbench file and Workbench opens with that project ready.

---

## Getting your work made

Everything comes out of the **Make It** button.

- **Cut List** — prints a list of the boards to buy, with every length on it.
  Take it to the timber yard.
- **3D printer file (STL)** — send this to a 3D printer to have the piece
  printed.
- **Shop drawing (DXF)** — the flat drawing a fabrication shop needs if you
  want a part cut for you. Email them this file.

---

## If something goes wrong

Close Workbench and open it again. Your work will still be there.
